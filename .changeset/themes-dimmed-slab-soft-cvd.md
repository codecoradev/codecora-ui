Add four themes with range beyond colour: `dimmed` (low-glare mid-grey dark with quieter accents), `slab` (brutalist print: square corners, mono headings, hard offset shadows), `soft` (round corners, rounded sans, thin icons) and `neutral-cvd` (`neutral` with blue success and vermillion danger for red/green colour vision deficiency). Themes gain an optional `shadow` for floating layers; `--cc-shadow-popover` and `--cc-shadow-modal` now come from the theme and are listed in `semanticTokens`.
