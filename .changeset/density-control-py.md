A fifth density token, `--cc-control-py`, sets the block padding of rows whose label can wrap: 0.5rem at comfortable (unchanged) and 0.25rem at compact. Checkbox, RadioGroup, Switch and Accordion rows use it, so at compact density a one-line row is 32 px like every other control instead of 36 to 40 px.
