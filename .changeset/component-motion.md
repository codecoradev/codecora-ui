Motion for the components that still cut between states, on the existing motion tokens. Tooltip, DropdownMenu, Select and Combobox fade and scale in from their trigger and out on close; CommandPalette fades and drops in like Dialog. Accordion panels open to their measured height and the chevron turns. The Tabs underline slides to the selected tab. Checkbox fills and its check mark springs in. Slider keyboard steps glide and the thumb grows while held, with no lag while dragging. ToggleGroup and Pagination ease their fill and colour. Avatar images fade in once loaded, and Alert fades in when it appears. Under `prefers-reduced-motion` every one of them is instant.
