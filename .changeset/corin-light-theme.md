New `corin-light` theme: Corin's warm-paper light palette as the light twin of `corin`, with the same teal accent, radii, fonts and shape hooks (`data-theme="corin-light"`, `themes/corin-light.css`). It passes WCAG AA for every accent. Corin's own light red, green, amber and muted border fail AA, so those four are darkened at Corin's hue angles; `docs/design/corin-light.md` lists them. `corin` now names `corin-light` as its `twin` (#129).
